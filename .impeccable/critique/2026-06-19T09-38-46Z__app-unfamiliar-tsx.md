## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**Not AI slop.** Screen substantively clean. Problem options read as authored by someone with lived knowledge of what drivers experience, not generated by pattern-matching on "safety app" training data. Clarifiers ("Something about this area feels wrong — I want to leave") are precise without being clinical. Phantom-chevron placeholder trick thoughtful detail. Code comments referencing user-flagged decisions and design rationale are healthy counter-signal.

Minor slop-adjacent risk: aspirationalNote on destination step ("Pick a safe destination and we'll route you there. Your contact already has a text draft in Messages.") packs two different reassurances into one footnote-weight line. In a charged moment these should be foregrounded or separated.

## Emotional Journey

**Step 1 — Problem Picker:** Screen opens into generous white space with eyebrow "Ok. You're somewhere unfamiliar." — "Ok." grounding beat that acknowledges moment without amplifying. Three problem cards with clarifiers invite recognition rather than demanding precision. Lifted white cards with soft shadow read as safe and composed, not clinical. **Acknowledged, not panicked.**

Gap: once user picks problem, screen transitions to step 2 with no verbal acknowledgment of specific choice. "I'm being followed" deserves beat of "Got it — let's move." Current transition is silent.

**Step 2 — Destination Picker:** "Let's get you someplace safe." is right register. Aspirational note ("Pick a safe destination and we'll route you there") instructional-correct but emotionally flat at 13pt/labelSecondary. Second clause ("Your contact already has a text draft in Messages") is more important reassurance and visually subordinate.

"I'm safe now" outline button at bottom — in this step, before anything has been done to make them safe, phrasing is functionally premature.

**Active Session View:** "Already on it." is closest screen gets to warmth, lands well. "Sharing in Unfamiliar area." technically correct but stiff — "Unfamiliar area" reads as data label, not empathetic copy. Session reason echoed as "Reason: I'm being followed" is clinical restatement of user's own words.

## What's Working

**1. Problem clarifiers as recognition scaffolding.** Three-word title plus one-sentence clarifier pattern is exactly right design for stress-state triage UI. A frightened user does not have to name their situation precisely — they can recognize it.

**2. Session re-entry guard.** Lazy initialization pattern for `step` state correctly prevents returning user from being dropped back to problem picker and overwriting active session. Code comment explaining why ("We'd land on 'problem' and let the user start a NEW session that overwrites the existing one's startedAtIso") is institutional memory.

**3. Contact-less graceful degradation.** `hasLifeline` gate hiding `NotifyingPulse` when no contact set is right call. Code comment explains rationale ("the pulse copy literally claims notification, so it must hide when there's no one to notify rather than degrade silently"). Honest disclosure.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Silent async during destination search — no loading state**
- What: `handleDestinationPick` requests location permissions, gets GPS position, runs POI search with no visible loading indicator. UI remains fully interactive and static during what could be 2-5 second operation.
- Why it matters: In safety flow, non-responsive interface during charged moment breaks trust immediately. User does not know if tap registered, if app froze, or if something went wrong. May tap again, triggering duplicate requests. Highest-impact gap on screen.
- Fix: Disable tapped destination card and show loading indicator from moment `handleDestinationPick` called until `router.replace` fires or error Alert shows. Consider `loadingId` state tracking which destination option is loading.

**[P0] Error recovery via system Alert breaks composed safety register**
- What: All three error paths (location denied, no results, search failed) fire `Alert.alert` — modal native alert that drops user out of sheet context, displays in generic system dialog, returns them to destination picker with no in-surface state.
- Why it matters: System Alert jarring in flow designed to feel calm and composed. User returns to destination picker with no memory of what just happened — no in-surface copy explaining "Location access was denied" or "No gas stations found nearby."
- Fix: Replace Alerts with in-surface feedback. Inline error states below destination list (small footnote-weight, `labelTertiary`) explaining what happened and what to try. Keep Alert for true "this device cannot do X" case.

**[P1] "I'm safe now" on step 2 is one unguarded tap away from ending active safety session**
- What: "I'm safe now" outline button on DestinationPicker ends session and navigates away via `handleSafeNow`, which calls `endSession()`. One accidental tap while scrolling destination list terminates session.
- Why it matters: Session protects user. Ending it accidentally, in moment where they are actively scared and mid-flow, is consequential. No confirmation, no undo.
- Fix: Single-step confirmation — either `Alert.alert('End sharing?', 'Your contact will no longer be notified.', [...])`, or — more on-brand — subtle "Hold to end" or double-tap-to-confirm pattern.

**[P1] No acknowledgment of selected problem during step transition**
- What: After user picks "I'm being followed" and session starts, step transitions immediately to DestinationPicker with generic "Let's get you someplace safe." subtitle. Specific problem vanishes.
- Why it matters: User just disclosed something frightening. App's response should acknowledge what they said before pivoting to logistics. For Black driver who picked "I feel unsafe" because surroundings felt wrong, pivot to "where do you want to go?" without intervening "We've got you" beat can read as dismissive.
- Fix: Carry selected problem into DestinationPicker as prop and use in subtitle: "You're being followed. Let's move." or "You feel unsafe. Let's get you somewhere better." `sessionReason` already available in `useShareSession()`.

**[P2] ActiveSessionView copy is clinically registered**
- What: "Sharing in Unfamiliar area." reads as status log entry, not human voice. "Reason: I'm being followed" is label-value pair, not empathetic restatement.
- Why it matters: This is screen user sees if mid-session and re-opened app. Likely still in charged situation. Brand voice should be most audible here.
- Fix: Rewrite title: "We're with you." or "Location is shared." Reason line should be human: "You said: [sessionReason]" warmer than "Reason: [sessionReason]" — or drop label entirely: "Sharing because you felt followed."

**[P2] Lifeline modal subtitle makes capability claim flow doesn't always back**
- What: LifelineModal subtitle: "Your Trusted Contact is alerted during emergencies and can see your current location." Present-tense capability statement may be imprecise depending on `useShareSession` implementation.
- Why it matters: Principle 4 of PRODUCT.md — honesty of disclosure. If contact has Messages draft but is not actually receiving live location, "can see your current location" is a lie. Dangerous: user may take risks because they believe live oversight is in place when it is not.
- Fix: Audit `useShareSession` against this copy. If location sharing is draft/manual-send model rather than live-push, change copy: "Your Trusted Contact has a ready-to-send message with your location."

**[P3] backChevron tap-target alignment not painted at 44pt**
- What: backChevron is `{ width: 44, height: 44, alignItems: 'flex-start', justifyContent: 'center' }`. Icon left-aligned within 44pt box — icon's visual edge at container's left edge. Painted glyph occupies only its natural ~28pt width.
- Why it matters: `.cursorrules` explicit: "44×44 pt minimum on the visual, not just the hit area." Rule violation, not polish nit.
- Fix: Change to `alignItems: 'center'` and constrain container to icon's left edge. Or use `tapTarget44` token.

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (accessibility):**
`pulseFooter` on ActiveSessionView wraps NotifyingPulse in View — `NotifyingPulse` only renders as Pressable when `onPress` passed. `onResendSms` is passed here, so inner component tappable. But outer View (`minHeight: 44`) has no accessibility role or label. For VoiceOver users, `pulseFooter` View not announced. accessibilityLabel on problem cards includes both title and clarifier in one label — correct. Dynamic Type wrapped via `dynamicType()` throughout — strong baseline.

**Casey (distracted mobile):**
Destination picker has three cards stacked with 16pt gap. On standard iPhone (390pt wide), each card ~60pt tall. Three stacked cards scannable in one pass. Icon-plus-text pattern distinguishes destinations faster than text alone. However, "I'm safe now" button at very bottom — on small or short iPhone (SE, mini) Casey may have to scroll. No sticky bottom — button scrolls with content.

**Black driver assessing safety in a charged moment:**
Screen's register well-calibrated — three problem options surface lived experience without clinical labeling, approach of "route me somewhere safe" rather than "call 911" respects thesis. Two critical gaps:

(1) Lifeline modal subtitle's "can see your current location" — if overstates live-location capability, driver who believes they have continuous oversight may make decisions (e.g., continuing through area rather than stopping) based on false premise. Stakes here are not UX polish — they are physical safety.

(2) Step 1 → step 2 transition with no acknowledgment of picked problem. Driver who just disclosed "I'm being followed" and immediately gets "Where do you want to go?" without intervening beat may experience app as transactional precisely when they need it to feel like companion.

## Minor Observations

- LifelineModal uses `borderTopLeftRadius: 20` inline; `radii.xl` (= 20) exists in `theme/radii.ts`.
- `iconRow` uses `minHeight: 60` inline magic number. Not on `safetyCardHeight` path; not tokenized.
- LifelineModal's DragHandle sits outside body View — spacing between top of card and drag handle versus drag handle and title may be asymmetric.
- `NotifyingPulse`'s accessibilityLabel on wrapping Pressable diverges from component's own default label — minor inconsistency.
- `handleSafeNow` catches errors with `console.warn` and silently returns. If `endSession()` fails, user has no idea session still active.

## Questions to Consider

1. What does "can see your current location" actually mean in current implementation? If `useShareSession` sends Messages draft rather than live-push, LifelineModal subtitle is overcommitting.
2. Should destination picker remember last-used or most-used destination across sessions?
3. Is there no-contact path UX worth designing? Currently whole pulseFooter and LifelineModal hide when no contact set; user may wonder if absence means something broken.
4. What happens if user picks "I'm being followed" and destination search returns no results? Alert fires and returns to destinations — but session has already started.
5. Drag handle implies bottom sheet. Is it? Screen routed via expo-router as full-screen route, not Modal. Drag handle signals "drag to dismiss" but no gesture handler — handle is cosmetic.
