## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: No AI-slop tells. The About-eyebrow addition is exactly the moment "labeled section above a card" earns its keep — the single-row About group was previously visually identical to the single-row Sign-out group, and the user had no semantic cue for which they were tapping; the eyebrow disambiguates without scaffolding the page. The carousel-below-config order is honest IA: substantive settings lead, onboarding/progressive-setup tiles are secondary discovery. Wiltedgreen RowGroup eyebrow + Phosphor Gear in wiltedgreen header glyph + carousel tile's wiltedgreen border = the brand voice carrying the chrome confidently per DESIGN.md's Wilted-Eyebrow Rule. Hero-Title Rule properly NOT triggered here (SettingsHeader large gear + "Settings" is a 28pt Title1, not the 34pt Large Title — Settings is a screen title, not the screen-that-is-the-screen).

**Deterministic scan**: detector returned 0 findings.

## Overall Impression
The layout restructure paid off in two principled ways: carousel demoted to discovery, About eyebrow disambiguates single-row groups. The screen is now the canonical example of iOS grouped-settings in this codebase — RowGroup + SettingsRow + SettingsHeader carrying the chrome, brand voice expressed through eyebrow color, profile card as identity peer above app-config. The standing 2026-06-19 gaps (no sign-out confirm, calendar-connect feedback invisible) are workflow/feature items, not visual ones.

## What's Working
- **About eyebrow.** Single-row group with an eyebrow caption disambiguates from the single-row Sign-out group below it. The reader knows what they're looking at without needing two competing visual treatments.
- **Carousel below app-config.** Onboarding/progressive discovery sits below substantive settings — honesty of disclosure (the tiles ARE secondary) + correct IA priority.
- **Wiltedgreen RowGroup eyebrow.** The Wilted-Eyebrow Rule applied here means the carousel's wiltedgreen tile border, the gear glyph in header, and the eyebrow text all share one brand voice across the settings register.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Sign-out clears identity with no confirmation.** Standing 2026-06-19 P1 issue. Promise.all over 7+ stores; one mis-tap = re-onboarding. **Fix**: confirmation Alert before triggering the cascade (iOS native Action Sheet with destructive style on the Sign-out option). **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden app/menu.tsx`
- **[P2] Calendar tile fire-and-forget with no feedback.** Tap fires `connectCalendar()` and the tile sits inert until next focus pass clears it via `showCalendarTile = false`. User has no signal that anything happened. **Fix**: loading state on the tile itself; on failure, surface the error inline. **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify app/menu.tsx`
- **[P3] No current-value surfacing on app-config rows except Saved places.** Refuel reminders, Zone Preferences, Safety would each benefit from a value snippet ("On", "3 categories on", "Trusted contact set"). **Fix**: pass `value` prop on each. **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish app/menu.tsx`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**: No keyboard shortcuts (not really applicable for mobile but Alex's mental model maps to "tap fewer times to know what's set"). Lack of current-value surfacing on rows means scanning the settings tree to find what's misconfigured requires tapping into each.

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: Carousel-below-config is the right call for Jordan too — substantive settings lead, progressive setup as secondary. The tile copy ("Tell us how often you fill up so you don't run low in an unsafe spot.") is exactly the brand voice this persona needs.
