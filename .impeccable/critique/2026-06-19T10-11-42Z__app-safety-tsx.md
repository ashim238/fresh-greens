## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**None of the four named anti-references are triggered.** This is one of the cleaner screens in the flow.

- No generic ride-share/SaaS drift. Palette, weight choices, tile layout read as intentional and community-oriented.
- No alarmist safety-app red. SOS bar uses red-burst `sidebtn-sos.svg` illustration inside grouped surface register — red stays illustrative, not ambient. Reserved-color rule respected. Navy on header shield correct per carve-out #6.
- No AI slop: no gradient text, glassmorphism, eyebrow labels on every section.
- No sterile/clinical: held-question register ("What's going on?") with Title1 Regular correct.

One soft concern: grid of four identically-sized tiles with centered illustration + label faintly echoes "identical card grids" anti-pattern. Saved by illustration distinctiveness.

## Cognitive Load

Low on entry; 2×2 with single-concept tiles as scannable as this screen gets. Load spikes in two moments:

1. **When share session is already active.** User has no ambient indicator of that state. Cross-tile conflict only discoverable by tapping wrong tile and reading Alert.
2. **Choosing between Unfamiliar area and Share location.** Both involve location and trusted contacts. Difference (routing aid vs. live share) not legible from tile labels alone.

SOS bar low-load by design.

## Emotional Journey

The intended arc — "I need help → I can pick the right kind of help → I'm in control" — lands for mid-scenario case. Friction at two emotional edges:

**Calm planning moment:** Modal opens over home or en-route. For user who has never used it, screen asks "What's going on?" with no orientation. Brand voice correct; information architecture assumes prior knowledge.

**Charged moment (most important case):** Four-tile picker is one extra tap before reaching relevant sub-flow. If user already in traffic stop and pressed safety FAB, question "What's going on?" appropriate — but Answer (Pulled-over) should be first tile, thumb-reachable, large. It is top-left, correct for LTR scanning.

SOS bar at bottom provides escape hatch for when wrong option showing. Visual separation and directional CaretRight chevron read correctly.

Brand personality — "Steady Companion, not panic button" — held throughout. Screen does not scream.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] Active session state invisible on picker**
- What: When share session already active, this screen shows no indicator. User only discovers state by tapping conflicting tile and receiving Alert: "You're in a Unfamiliar area session. End it first to enter Share location."
- Why it matters: Charged-moment user not reading Alerts — they are tapping. Alert that says "end it first" without way to end it from Alert is dead end.
- Fix: Show status badge or subtitle on active tile ("Active session"). Add "Resume" shortcut routing directly to active flow's screen.

**[P1] No-contact gate reactive, not proactive**
- What: "Share location" fires no-contact Alert on tap. Tile looks identical to any other fully-available tile until after tap.
- Why it matters: In stress moment, tap that fails and opens setup flow is disorienting.
- Fix: If `!contact`, render Share location tile with visual state that signals prerequisite (small "Set up" chip under label, or lock glyph on icon, or reduced opacity on illustration).

**[P2] Tile Pressables have no `accessibilityHint`; illustrations carry no alt text**
- What: Four grid tiles set `accessibilityLabel={tab.label}` but no `accessibilityHint`. VoiceOver reads "Pulled-over, button" with no explanation of what sub-flow does.
- Why it matters: Primary user group explicitly includes people who may be in high-stress moment, possibly in low-visibility conditions.
- Fix: Add `accessibilityHint` to each tile. Example: `"Opens guidance for a traffic stop — arms recording and prepares to contact your trusted contact."` for Pulled-over.

**[P2] Tile label copy — "Pulled-over" hyphen and "Unfamiliar area" ambiguity**
- What: "Pulled-over" hyphenated as if compound modifier before noun ("a pulled-over car"), but here stands alone as noun label. Grammatically correct standalone form is "Pulled Over" or "Traffic Stop". "Unfamiliar area" vs "Share location" semantically close.
- Why it matters: In moment requiring quick pattern-matching, incorrectly hyphenated label and semantically ambiguous pair slow parsing.
- Fix: Rename "Pulled-over" → "Pulled Over" or "Traffic Stop". Add one-line subtitles under each tile label.

**[P3] `emergencySectionLabel` at 13pt footnoteRegular / labelTertiary may be missed**
- What: "Need help now?" set in `typography.footnoteRegular` (13pt) at `colors.labelTertiary`.
- Why it matters: As label introducing most critical escape hatch on screen, 13pt tertiary gray quiet for its semantic weight.
- Fix: Bump to `footnoteEmphasized` (13pt, weight 600) or `subheadlineRegular` (15pt).

## Persona Red Flags

**Sam (accessibility):**
Four tile Pressables have VoiceOver labels but no hints. Sam navigating by swipe hears "Pulled-over, button" — and because illustration correctly set `accessible={false}`, swipe reading gives no additional context. Sam cannot distinguish Unfamiliar area from Share location from announcement alone. SOS bar sets better standard tiles should match.

**Casey (distracted mobile):**
Four-tile picker one tap deep. On muscle memory, fine. For first-time use in charged moment, "What's going on?" with four options reads as question Casey has to stop and think about. Tile Casey needs most in traffic stop (Pulled-over) is top-left — correct placement. SOS bar thumb-reachable at bottom. Main risk for Casey: reactive no-contact gate.

**Black driver assessing safety in a charged moment:**
Two specific concerns:

First, modal header says "Safety" with shield icon and "What's going on?" — correct register. Does not alarm; asks. Brand promise ("Steady Companion") held at opening. Genuine strength.

Second, and more acutely: driver who has been pulled over and taps "Pulled-over" making real decision with real stakes. If they have cross-tile session conflict, they receive Alert rather than guidance. Alert copy reads bureaucratically — "End it first to enter Share location." — in moment when user is likely stressed. If Alert fires during traffic stop, driver is looking at alert dialog in view of law enforcement officer.

## Minor Observations

- `SidebtnSafety width={32} height={32}` inside 56×56 iconBox: icon is 32/56 = 57% of box. Leaves visible dead space.
- `root` style sets `borderTopLeftRadius: 28` and `borderTopRightRadius: 28` as inline literal values. `radii.xl` is 20pt; 28pt not on scale.
- `subtitle` text ("What's going on?") uses `typography.bodyRegular` (17pt) in `labelTertiary`. Given that "What's going on?" is primary prompt, bodyRegular at tertiary gray quiet.
- Comment on `title` style (SAF1) detailed and useful, but SAF prefix numbering system not documented anywhere.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should Safety modal have close button? Drag handle decorative; on first use invisible.
2. Can Pulled-over be promoted to primary CTA rather than grid tile? Majority of most consequential safety interactions run through Pulled-over flow.
3. When share session active, should Safety modal open directly into active sub-flow instead of showing picker?
4. Does "Need help now?" label add clarity or clutter? Hairline separator already divides emergency lane from toolkit.
5. What does Safety modal look like for user who has not completed onboarding? Pulled-over and Roadside work without contact. Unfamiliar area hides lifeline pulse. Share location fires Alert.
