## Anti-Patterns Verdict: PASS

No AI slop. Cleared: side-stripe borders, gradient text, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates, identical card grids, uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section markers. Phosphor icons exclusively (0 non-Phosphor). Zero legacy Touchables. DM Serif used at 6 payoff moments only — correct restraint. Design reads as authored, not generated.

## What's Working

1. **Reserved-color discipline is real, not aspirational.** Across 20 screens + 307 a11y attributes, orange/red/yellow/navy appear only in semantic roles. Zero decorative uses. Hardest design-system rule to enforce — it holds.
2. **The Held-Question Rule creates a distinct emotional register.** Regular-weight "What's going on?" / "What's the situation?" holds space open rather than commanding — a Fresh Greens invention serving the thesis. An app for Black drivers navigating police encounters cannot bark commands.
3. **The warm surface ramp earns its complexity.** Five surfaces each have a distinct role (page/card/sheet/elevated/tinted). OKLCH at 0.008–0.012 chroma reads "not cold" rather than "warm" — right calibration for a safety app.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] StateCard `width: 326` overflows iPhone SE** — `components/StateCard.tsx:148`. Hardcoded width exceeds SE's 320pt logical width. Used in 6 surfaces (search, recordings, zone-preferences, trusted-contact-setup, RoadsideTowPick, RoutePreviewCard). Fix: `maxWidth: 326` + `width: '100%'` or `alignSelf: 'stretch'` + horizontal margin. → `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] `colors.red` used for error text instead of `colors.severityCritical` (11 sites)** — Most widespread inconsistency. severityCritical (#C62828) is the WCAG-AA foreground token; red (#FF3B30) is the alert signal and has lower contrast on warm backgrounds. Sites: login:243, get-started:246, moderation:1000/1025, share-location:298, unfamiliar:503/508, report:1342, SafetyErrorMessage:57, RecordingSaveErrorBanner:108, RoadsideTowPick:261, RowGroup:118. Leave red on true signals (SOS, destructive labels). → `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Four phantom empty-onPress Pressables for modal tap-swallowing** — LifelineModal:75, LiveSafetySheet:154, RouteComparisonSheet:63, FuelStopsSheet:135. VoiceOver announces a tappable button that does nothing. Fix: `View` + `onStartShouldSetResponder={() => true}`. → `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Two fixed-width buttons break under Dynamic Type** — sign-out:152 and permissions:348 both `width: 163`. Won't accommodate text growth. Fix: `minWidth: 163` + horizontal padding. → `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] Math-only 44pt compliance with no floor** — HomeBrowseSheet chips (895), trip-summary retry lines (406/427), NotifyingPulse. Compute to exactly 44pt but Dynamic Type can drop below. Fix: add `minHeight: 44`. → `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Help & documentation is the weakest heuristic (2.5)** — No contextual help beyond onboarding. "Fuel day cycle" / "Tank range" unexplained for first-timers. → `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Report submission errors block instead of recover inline** — report.tsx uses Alert.alert; recordings/moderation/share-location use inline `accessibilityLiveRegion` banners. Inconsistent + higher stress. → `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Three hand-rolled pill buttons duplicate Button component** — RoutePreviewCard (667/741), HomePlacementOverlay (37), RoadsideTowPick (189). Drift risk. → `/impeccable distill`

## Questions to Consider

1. Is the Held-Question Rule too subtle for users who need directive guidance under real adrenaline? Has it been stress-tested?
2. Does the warm surface ramp survive direct sunlight? Surface-to-surface contrast is minimal at ~97% lightness; no high-contrast mode. Production driving app → P0; thesis → acceptable.
3. Should moderation be a web dashboard instead of an in-app screen? It's the only admin surface and breaks the emotional register.
4. Would removing all 6 DM Serif moments change how "branded" the app feels? Is invisible-brand-as-trustworthy-tool the intent, or should the serif reach the home greeting?
