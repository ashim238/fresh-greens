## Overall Impression
The layout restructure paid off: this is the screen the calm-companion brand voice was made for, and the chips-above-metadata sequence finally serves the thesis claim ("show your work") at the IA level, not just at the chip-level. The biggest remaining gap is the long compound conditional governing what renders in the route-preview body — three branches (`isCalculatingRoute` / `routeFetchSource === 'no-route'` / default), nested chip-row variants (failed / present / all-clear), conditional safety lines (trustedOnRouteRow, suggestedDeparture). At AX5 + long destination name + 3 hazard chips + safe chips + trusted-station line + suggested departure, the card stack approaches a wall of text. The card knows it: it has `numberOfLines={1}` + `adjustsFontSizeToFit` + min-scale 0.7 on minutes already, which is admitting the constraint. The next pass is probably consolidation, not addition.

## What's Working
- **Chips above ETA materially clarified the screen.** Before, the eye landed on "12 min · arrive 8:30" then had to scan down for safety chips; now the safety story is the first read, ETA is the answer to the safety question. This is IA-thesis convergence.
- **Hero collapsed into a tight 4pt group.** routeSummaryBlock holding title + minutes + arrival + distance reads as one unit instead of three competing rows — the prior conditions caption was deleted entirely, which was the right call.
- **Route-cycle chevrons stayed in the top row, not the headline.** The 2026-06-03 learnings note documented this; the directional slide-in (lastCycleDirRef → routeShiftX) is the canonical Calm-Physics example.

## Priority Issues

- **[P2] Long route-preview vertical stack at AX5.** Title + hero row + chips header + chip row + safe chips + via row + trustedOnRouteRow + suggestedDeparture footnote. Each is reasonable in isolation; together at AX5 with a 4-word destination and 3 hazard chips, the card overflows or scrolls past the natural fold. **Fix**: audit the worst-case stack at AX5 on iPhone SE in a screenshot, then decide whether trustedOnRouteRow + suggestedDeparture should fold into a single "Extras" disclosure. **Suggested command**: `/impeccable distill app/home.tsx`
- **[P2] FAB stack uses magic-number bottom math.** `fabAnchorHeight + 24 + 56 + 12` for Recenter, `fabAnchorHeight + 24` for Report. Read in three places; not factored. **Fix**: extract a `fabStackOffsets(anchorHeight)` helper or two named tokens. **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish app/home.tsx`

Live testing earlier showed users needed the hint; this confirms it stays, but the copy could collapse to one line. **Fix**: "Tap to move.
