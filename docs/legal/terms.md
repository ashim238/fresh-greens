## Use the app safely

- In a genuine emergency, **call 911 directly.** Do not use Fresh Greens as a substitute for emergency services.

Treat the indicator as a *commitment to share*, not as confirmation that your contact is actively viewing your location.
- The Roadside Assistance flow lets you dial your roadside service directly. We do not negotiate, verify, or guarantee anything about the service you call — they are responsible for their own response.
- The Pulled-over recording is for your personal record. It is not automatically transmitted to anyone. Whether you share it with your attorney, friends, or anyone else is your decision.
